How CONNECT-TO-WORK helps the community

1. CONNECT

Build useful relationships through community networking, mentorship, peer learning and responsible referrals.

2. DEVELOP

Strengthen employability through skills practice, a career goal, a résumé roadmap and a preferred-language profile.

3. EMPLOY

Explore job profiles, local opportunities and employer-submitted vacancies that are reviewed before publication.

National Employment & Safety Hotlines

DOLE Hotline 15/30 — local employment and labor concerns

Dial 1-5-3-0. This 24/7 line replaced the former DOLE 1349 hotline in November 2026.

📞 Call DOLE 15/30 Official DOLE notice
Department of Migrant Workers (DMW) — overseas employment concerns

1348 in Metro Manila; (02) 1348 for regional callers using mobile phones; +632 1348 from overseas.

📞 Call DMW (02) 1348 Official DMW website
